02 · Challenge

Problem Statement

An automated drafting assistant is needed to reproduce patterns on a flat surface. The system must translate planned motion into coordinated movement of a robotic arm and produce a drawing with sufficient precision to make the intended shape recognisable. The challenge lies in achieving smooth, controlled and repeatable movement while working within the mechanical limitations of the arm, i.e., only using Revolute Joints. The Drafting assistant is atleast required to make basic shapes like a perfect circle, square and a triangle
